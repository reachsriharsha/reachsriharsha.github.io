## What I Can Do For You

### 1. Free Business Health Check

A one-hour conversation (in person or on a call) where I understand how your business runs day to day — orders, stock, staff, payments, customers. You get a short written list of:
- What can be simplified or automated
- What it will roughly cost and how much time it saves
- What to do first

Even if you never work with me, you will leave with ideas you can use.

### 2. Google Sheets and Excel Made Useful

Most small businesses do not need new software. They need their existing sheets to work properly.
- Clean, simple sheets for orders, stock, expenses, attendance, or customers
- Drop-down lists and checks so staff cannot enter wrong data
- Automatic totals, daily and monthly summaries, and simple charts
- Google Forms so staff or customers fill data from their phones, straight into your sheet
- Automatic reminders — low stock, payment due, renewal due, follow-up pending
- Automatic daily or weekly summary sent to your email or phone

### 3. Everyday Automation

Stop doing the same thing by hand every day.
- **Invoices and quotations** generated from a sheet in one click, as PDF, ready to share
- **Payment reminders** to customers on WhatsApp, SMS, or email
- **Order collection** from customers through a simple form or WhatsApp
- **Appointment booking and reminders** for clinics, salons, tutors, and service businesses
- **Staff reporting** — daily sales, attendance, or site updates collected in one place automatically
- **Supplier and purchase tracking** so you know what was ordered, received, and paid

### 4. Custom Software Built Only For You

When sheets are not enough, I build small, focused apps that fit your exact workflow — nothing more, nothing less.
- Simple mobile-friendly apps your staff can use on their phones
- Billing, inventory, and order management for your specific products
- Customer records and follow-up tracking
- Job cards, work orders, and production tracking for workshops and small factories
- Student, batch, and fee management for coaching centres and schools
- Patient records and appointment management for clinics
- Dashboards showing today's sales, pending payments, and stock at a glance

You own what is built. It is designed so you are not locked in to me or to an expensive monthly subscription.

### 5. AI Assistants That Actually Help

AI is no longer only for big companies. Practical uses for a small business:
- A WhatsApp assistant that answers common customer questions (timings, prices, availability) in Kannada, Hindi, or English
- Reading bills, invoices, and handwritten notes from photos and entering them into your sheet
- Drafting replies, product descriptions, social media posts, and quotations
- Summarising daily sales or feedback in plain language

I only suggest AI where it saves real time or money — not because it is fashionable.

### 6. Teaching You and Your Staff

A tool is useless if nobody uses it. Every engagement includes hands-on training until you and your team are comfortable.
- Google Sheets and Forms basics for owners and staff
- Using WhatsApp Business properly — catalogues, labels, quick replies, broadcast lists
- Using AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude for everyday business work
- Short refresher sessions when new staff join

Training is done in the language your team is most comfortable with.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**My business is very small. Is this for me?**
Yes. Some of the most useful work is a single well-made Google Sheet. Small is exactly who this is for.

**I am not good with computers or phones. Will I be able to use it?**
Yes. Everything is designed for the people who will actually use it, and I train you until you are comfortable.

**Do I need to buy new computers or expensive software?**
Usually not. Most work uses your existing phone, a free Google account, and WhatsApp.

**What if something stops working later?**
Call or message me. Small fixes are handled quickly, and monthly support is available if you want ongoing help.

**Will my business data be safe?**
Your data stays in your own Google account or system. I will explain who has access, and you can remove my access at any time.

**Can you work with my accountant or existing billing software (like Tally)?**
Yes. Often the goal is to get clean data to your accountant or billing software with less manual typing.
